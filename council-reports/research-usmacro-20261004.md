## Diagnosis: where the US sits in Dalio's short-term debt cycle (≈230 words)

**Phase: late-cycle / "tightening" — the stage where the central bank, having let an expansion run with inflation above target, begins withdrawing liquidity, and where growth is still above potential but financial conditions are turning restrictive.** Note the unusual sequencing: the 2024–25 easing (6 cuts to 3.50–3.75%) was reversed in Sep 2026 by the first hike since 2023 under a new, hawkish chair, triggered by an exogenous oil shock layered on a positive output gap and sticky 3% core PCE.

**Strongest evidence for late-cycle/tightening:** (1) Capacity and prices: positive output gap (>1%), ISM prices 77.9, PPI 5.4% y/y, core PCE 3.0% after revisions, 1-yr inflation expectations 4.6%, and the Fed hiking with 12/18 dots favoring more. (2) Financial conditions biting at the margin: 10y 5.28% (highest since 2007), mortgage 7.28%, MOVE 107, HY OAS widening 266→324bp in ten days, Russell −7.5% in Q3, confidence at 2014 lows. (3) Classic late-cycle labor signature: unemployment stable/low (4.2%), claims near 57-yr lows, but hiring stalled (+29k, JOLTS "limited dynamism") and real wages negative.

**Weakest evidence (arguing it is not yet pre-recession):** (1) Curve steeply positive (2s10s +44bp, 3m10y +111bp), NY Fed probability ~16%, Sahm 0.0, LEI 6-mo −0.1% — no recession trigger. (2) Growth momentum strong: Q3 nowcasts 2.3–3.7%, retail control +1.4%, services PMI 58.7, record 15.7% margins, EPS +29% — an AI-capex-plus-fiscal (OBBBA, deficit ~6.5% GDP) impulse insulating demand. (3) Credit/debt service healthy: HHD DSR ~11%, IG 86bp, bank credit +6%, SLOOS easing for C&I.

**Archetype implication for the next 6–12 months:** the Fed tightens further (market: ~4.6–4.7% by H2 2027) until asset prices and credit growth crack; equities at CAPE 41 and ~0% ERP are the most vulnerable link. Expect a sequence of: further hikes → wider HY/private-credit spreads → slower hiring → the first negative payroll quarter and a Sahm trigger, with "early recession" becoming the modal phase by H2 2027 unless the oil shock reverses and lets the Fed stop at 4.00–4.25%.
