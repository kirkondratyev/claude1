**Timeline (all 2026):**
- **28 Feb** — US and Israel launch ~900 strikes in 12 hours ("Operation Epic Fury"); Supreme Leader Khamenei killed. Iran retaliates against US bases, Gulf states (Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan) and oil infrastructure. (Wikipedia/Britannica summaries)
- **4 Mar** — Iran closes Strait of Hormuz; QatarEnergy declares force majeure; Ras Laffan attacked (~$20bn damage, ~20% of Qatar LNG capacity lost for years). (Wikipedia economic-impact page; Stimson)
- **19 Mar** — US aerial campaign to reopen Hormuz. **13 Apr** — US naval blockade of Iranian ports. **5 May** — Epic Fury formally concluded; **7 May** further US strikes.
- **17 Jun** — US–Iran memorandum signed, "dual blockade" lifted; **28 Jun** cessation of attacks; **8 Jul** ceasefire collapses after Iranian attacks on commercial vessels.
- **Aug** — Iran strikes ≥13 commercial vessels; US escorts convoys and maintains blockade. (CNN 8 Sep)
- **4–8 Sep** — IRGC ballistic missiles fired at a US carrier/destroyer; US disables three Iranian tankers (Downy, Stark 1, Kylo); ≥7 tankers hit in a week; Brent back above $100 for first time since July. (NPR 5 Sep; USNI 4 Sep; CNN 8 Sep)
- **10–11 Sep** — Houthis take Mokha and islands near Bab el-Mandeb; Iraqi militia attacks on Saudi East-West pipeline force shutdown (~4 mb/d had been rerouted via Yanbu); partial restart announced 23 Sep; France (24 Sep) and UK (22 Sep) pledge forces to protect Yanbu. - **Mid/late Sep** — US signals openness to talks (JKM eases); 22 Sep "peace breakthrough hopes" sell-off; 24 Sep "diplomacy vs threats". (Sprague Energy daily notes)
- **4 Oct** — Iran parliament speaker: strait stays shut until seven demands (sanctions, frozen assets, military pressure) are met. (discoveryalert/TE summary)

## Diagnosis (Dalio short-term debt-cycle archetype, world ex-US)

The world ex-US is in the **late-expansion / tightening phase** (Dalio's stage where growth is still positive but central banks are raising rates into rising inflation, long rates break higher, credit growth starts to roll and asset prices stop rising) — but it is a *supply-shock* late cycle, not a classic demand-overheating one, and the regions are desynchronised:

- **Eurozone/UK/Nordics/Australia:** late cycle, tightening. Headline inflation 3.8% (EZ) and 3.1%→4% (UK) is ~80% energy; core is only 2.5%, yet the ECB has hiked twice, the Fed/BoJ/RBA/Norges joined, unemployment is edging up (6.4%), new business lending halved in August, and sovereign stress (France 150bp, gilts 5.4%) is the long-end telling policy it is over-tightening into a fiscal problem.
- **Japan:** mid-to-late cycle normalisation — wages +4.7%, real wages positive, BoJ at 1.25%, JGBs at a 30-year high, equities +37% YTD.
- **Korea/Taiwan/India:** AI-and-export boom, late cycle (BoK two hikes, RBI about to hike); Korea's chip-stock correction (−23%) is the first crack.
- **China:** its own deleveraging cycle (stage 5–6): credit demand collapsed (new loans ¥60bn), growth below target, property in year five of decline; PPI reflation is imported energy, not demand.
- **Brazil:** early easing; **Russia/Gulf:** war-shock contraction with stagflation.

**Strongest evidence for late-cycle:** (1) seven major central banks hiking in Q3 and the rate-cut cycle declared over; (2) long rates at multi-decade highs (UST 5.3%, JGB 3.1%, gilt 5.4%, OAT 5.0%) with Global Agg −2.3% YTD; (3) credit and asset momentum turning — EZ new lending halved, China loans collapse, HY spreads widening off 12-month lows, gold −7.5% m/m, EAFE −3% in September.

**Weakest / contrary evidence:** (1) activity is still accelerating — global manufacturing PMI 53.0 (55-month high), EZ composite 53.1 (41-month high), WTO barometer 102; (2) the inflation is energy, not demand — core EZ 2.5%, Japan core 1.7%, China CPI 0.8% — so a Hormuz reopening would reverse it fast; (3) risk assets still up double digits YTD, spreads still tight (HY 324bp, EMBI 218bp), and AI demand is real (Nvidia +106%, Korea chips +263%).

**Next 6–12 months in the archetype:** tightening plus a 60–80% Hormuz supply shock should push growth below potential by H1 2027: rising unemployment in Europe, slower credit, an equity/credit correction led by the most levered AI financing (Oracle, CoreWeave, SoftBank/OpenAI), and continued long-end pressure on France/UK/Japan. Two branches: if the strait reopens (IEA assumes normalisation during 2027), energy inflation collapses, CBs pause by Q1–Q2 2027 and the cycle turns to easing with a shallow downturn; if it stays shut, a stagflationary squeeze with European recession risk and EM FX stress (dollar at 18-month high, UST >5%). China runs its own clock and will likely add RRR/rate cuts in Q4 2026 regardless.
