Where the council clashes

(a) Tenant stays: sell tenanted or active hold? Red Team and First Principles: list tenanted at ~2.5m now; the discount is a year's yield drag and liquidity vanishes in a bear case. Executor, backed by Decision Science: investors price at 6%+ gross (2.3–2.45m); a certain 130–390k discount exceeds the likely −3–5% drift, so sell-tenanted loses ~65k unless P(≥7% fall in 12 months) exceeds ~45%. Executor wins on arithmetic, once Expansionist's cash-for-keys (~one month's rent) is tried.

(b) Yield arbitrage or bear bet? Prosecutor: year-1 cash favours hold (+79k vs −38k), the 13–31k/yr spread takes 2–8 years to repay 60k friction plus tax, and at the +1–5% 2027 consensus four advisors backed the option their numbers lose. Defender concedes sell is partly a price view, but with no use-value exit friction is deferred, not avoided: over 24 months hold − sell ≈ 0.85×ΔP − 13k, breakeven is flat, and sell has higher EV once P(bear) exceeds 16%. Decision Science puts P(≥10% fall by Oct 2028) at ~30%, so the Defender wins narrowly, hence medium confidence. Unanswered Prosecutor point: Downtown is 86% built out, so 2027 supply is mostly JVC/Dubai South beta.

(c) The 12-month notice: free option or obligation? Red Team, Outsider and Executor call it a ~AED 1.5k free option; Expansionist is right that an eviction-for-sale notice obliges a sale (the tenant can claim compensation otherwise). Executor's mitigation (renew with the same tenant, never evict-then-relet) keeps it cheap. Serve it as a commitment to sell in October 2027.
