## 6. Selling costs and tenanted sale
Broker 2% + 5% VAT; Ellington NOC/SPA admin fee AED 5,250; trustee AED 4,000+VAT usually split 50/50; DLD 4% legally 2%/2% but by custom buyer pays all; DEWA/chiller clearance AED 200–1,500; mortgage release AED 1,290–1,560 + 1% early settlement capped AED 10,000 if applicable. At AED 2.7m: 56,700 + 5,250 + 2,100 + ~1,000 ≈ AED 65k (2.4%). Tenant: Art 25(2)(d) Law 26/2007 as amended by 33/2008 requires 12 months' notarised/registered notice to vacate for sale; Art 28 — sale does not end the lease, buyer inherits it (The National 18 Sep 2026). Transfer of a seller-served notice to the buyer is contested; RDC has required fresh notices. Tenanted units draw investors only and trade 5–15% below vacant (RECD). The October 2026 expiry is the owner's clean window: if the tenant leaves, sell vacant (no notice issue); re-letting now resets the 12-month clock.
